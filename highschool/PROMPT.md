# HIGHSCHOOL

Make a game called **Highschool**. It's **super similar to Kindergarten**: one school day that keeps repeating, missions you do for other kids, teachers with a dark, evil side who can kill you, and things you learn carrying over to the next day. It uses **our own characters, story and art**, not copied from Kindergarten.

## Take inspiration from *The Calling of Aidan*

This is set in the same world as *The Calling of Aidan*, with the same friend group, and it should feel like it. Take inspiration from it:

- **Same pixel art style:** sharp, clean pixels, the same character sprites and colours. Characters have two legs and detailed poses (leaning, punching, headbutting, crossed arms, hands on hips, filming on a phone) and facial expressions.
- **Same feel:** funny, then suddenly brutal, gory and dark, then funny again. Lots of cursing and banter, brutal deaths, real friendship drama and real romance.
- **Cutscenes act out exactly what the text says.** If a line says someone does something, you see them do it. If a line says someone is somewhere else, they aren't on screen.
- **Quests split into parts with breaks**, group quests where everyone works together, friendship bonds, collectibles, multiple endings, and a big post-game curtain call.
- **The same kind of music and sounds:** music for each area and mood, and voice blips when people talk.
- **The same quality of life:** a quest log with icons, a backlog of dialogue, settings, auto and skip buttons that don't cover the text, and saving.

## Teachers

There are more missions with the teachers, and they show the teachers' darker, evil side.

### Ms. Shermian
- She loves splitting seats apart. Splitting friends up in the seating plan is one of her missions.
- She's sometimes nice and sometimes HATES kids. Her moods flip almost instantly from sweet to furious.
- She threatens, then kills, a student or two.

### Ms. Sherman
- Not to be confused with Ms. Shermian.
- She's an old teacher, like a modern Principal Krupp, and a jackass to all her students.

### Ms. Loach
- She doesn't care what you do. So the kids do something horrendous, the worst thing possible, just to see what she does.

### A couple of other weird, sketchy teachers
- More teachers with their own creepy, dark sides and their own missions.

## Missions

- **Ms. Shermian's seating plan:** this one follows **Corey, Arjun, Alex, Dheva and Marcus**. She splits them up, they try to get back together, her mood flips between nice and murderous, and Arjun's mouth makes everything worse.
- **Ms. Sherman:** missions about surviving and getting back at the worst principal-style teacher in the school.
- **Ms. Loach:** the kids keep doing worse and worse things in her class to finally get a reaction.
- **The other sketchy teachers:** each one has dark missions of their own.
- **Sean and Stella:** their romance in person, and it should be amazing.
- **Corey:** his romance in person, including the version where he gets it in PE.
- **Each new character** (Angelo, Alex, Dheva, Marcus, Paul, Kenneth, Jacob) gets their own missions.
- **Lots more missions and content** with the whole friend group, the same way *The Calling of Aidan* did it.

## Graphics and everything else

- **Pixel art** just like *The Calling of Aidan*: crisp, clean, square pixels at every size, including close-ups. No smooth or blurry shapes.
- **Detailed backgrounds** for every part of the school: classrooms, corridors with lockers, the cafeteria, the gym and PE, the library, the principal's office, the toilets, the field and the bus loop.
- **Detailed poses and animations**, specific to each situation.
- **Gore** in the same style as *The Calling of Aidan*.
- **Music and sound effects** for every area and mood.
- **Playable on keyboard and on phone/touch**, with saving.
