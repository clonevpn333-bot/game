# License activation

A license key turns the trial into the full version. Activating needs an internet connection once; after that {P} works offline.

## Activate

1. Find the license key in your receipt email from Lemon Squeezy.
2. In {P}, open **Settings → License**.
3. Paste the key and choose **Activate**.

The app sends the key and this computer’s name to `{LICENSE_HOST}` and stores the activation in your operating system’s credential store. A Solo key activates on {SOLO.activations} computers and a Firm key on {FIRM.activations}.

## Move to another computer

Open **Settings → License** on the old computer and choose **Deactivate this computer…**. That frees the activation, and the same key works on the new computer. Deactivating needs the internet; if the old computer is offline, it stays activated until you try again.

## Error messages

- **This key has reached its device limit**: deactivate it on another computer from Settings → License, then try again.
- **That license key wasn’t recognized**: check that you pasted the whole key from your purchase email.
- **This license key has been disabled** or **has expired**: the purchase was refunded or the key was revoked. Contact support if that’s unexpected.
- **This key is for a different product**: the key belongs to another Lemon Squeezy product. Use the key from your {P} receipt.
- **Couldn’t reach the license server**: activation needs the internet once. Check the connection, or a firewall that blocks `{LICENSE_HOST}`.

## Working offline

After activation, {P} checks the key again at most every 14 days, and only when it’s online. A failed check, for any network reason, never deactivates the app. Only an answer from the license server that the key was disabled, refunded or expired ends the license, and the app tells you when that happens.

## The trial

Until a key is activated, {P} exports binders for {TRIAL} different clients or tax years. Exporting the same client and year again doesn’t count twice. Trial binders carry a small “Created with {P} trial” note on the cover. Activating doesn’t change your clients, settings or history.
