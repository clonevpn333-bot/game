# Binder templates

Your firm’s binder order and naming template apply to every binder. Set them in **Settings → Binders**, or change them in the export dialog, which saves them the same way. The review board shows documents in the same order.

## Default category order

Binders follow this order until you change it:

1. Client organizer & engagement
2. Wages: W-2, W-2G
3. Interest & dividends: 1099-INT, 1099-OID, 1099-DIV
4. Brokerage: 1099-B, brokerage consolidated statements
5. Retirement & Social Security: 1099-R, SSA-1099, RRB-1099, 5498
6. Self-employment & other income: 1099-NEC, 1099-MISC, 1099-K, 1099-G
7. Health: 1099-SA, 1095-A, 1095-B, 1095-C, 5498-SA
8. Education: 1099-Q, 1098-T, 1098-E
9. Mortgage & property: 1099-S, 1098, property tax statements
10. Pass-through: Schedule K-1 (Form 1065, 1120-S, 1041)
11. Debt & other: 1099-C
12. Other / unrecognized

To change it, drag categories in **Settings → Binders → Default binder order** or in the export dialog’s **Binder order** list; **Reset to default order** puts it back. Empty categories are left out of the binder.

## Naming template

Document names are used for the bookmarks, the index and the individual files. They are built from four tokens:

- `{year}`: the tax year printed on the form
- `{form}`: the form type, such as `1099-DIV`
- `{issuer}`: the employer, payer or lender
- `{client}`: the client’s last name

The default template is `{year} {form} – {issuer} – {client}`, which produces names like `2026 1099-DIV – Sample Brokerage – Sample`. When {P} can’t read a value, the name leaves that part out rather than guess. Names you typed yourself on the review board stay as they are. The export dialog previews the template on the selected document.

## Cover page and index

Every binder can start with a cover page (your firm name and logo, the client, the tax year, the date and the document and page counts) and an index that links to each document. Turn either off in the export dialog. Add your firm name and logo (PNG or SVG) in **Settings → Firm profile**.

## Image quality

Scanned and photographed pages are saved at the quality you choose: **Compact** for email, **Balanced** (the default), or **High**. Digital PDFs are copied as they are, at full quality, whatever you pick.
