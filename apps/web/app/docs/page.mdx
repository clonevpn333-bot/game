# Getting started

This guide takes you from installing {P} to exporting your first client binder. It takes about ten minutes, and you can follow it with real client files: nothing leaves your computer.

## Install

Download the installer for your computer from the [download page](/download/):

- **Windows**: run the `.exe` installer. If Windows SmartScreen shows a warning for a new release, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag {P} into Applications.

The app opens in trial mode, with every feature, for {TRIAL} client binders. There is no account to create.

## Build your first binder

1. Choose **New binder** (or press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> on Windows, <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> on a Mac, and type “new”).
2. Pick an existing client or create one with a display name and tax year.
3. Drag the client’s files or folders onto the window. {P} accepts PDF, JPG, PNG, HEIC and multi-page TIFF files.

The processing view lists each file as it moves through reading, splitting and recognizing. Digital PDFs are read from their text layer; scans and photos are read with on-device text recognition.

## Review

The review board groups documents by binder category. High-confidence documents carry no marker. An amber dot means **Check**; a red dot means **Needs review**, and those documents sit at the top.

Work through the board with the keyboard: <Kbd>J</Kbd> and <Kbd>K</Kbd> move between documents and <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> accepts. The [review shortcuts](/docs/review-shortcuts/) page lists every key.

## Export

Choose **Export** to open the export dialog. The defaults produce one binder with a cover page, a clickable index and bookmarks. You can also export individually renamed files and pick an output folder, which {P} remembers per client.

When the export finishes, choose **Show in Explorer** (Windows) or **Show in Finder** (macOS) to open the folder.
