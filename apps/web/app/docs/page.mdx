# Getting started

This guide takes you from installing {P} to exporting your first client binder. It takes about ten minutes, and you can follow it with real client files: nothing leaves your computer.

## Install

Download the installer for your computer from the [download page](/download/):

- **Windows**: if your browser says the file isn’t commonly downloaded, keep it (in Edge: **⋯ → Keep**, then **Show more → Keep anyway**). Run the `.exe` installer. If a blue “Windows protected your PC” box appears, choose **More info**, then **Run anyway**.
- **macOS**: open the `.dmg` and drag {P} into Applications. The first time you open it, macOS says it can’t verify the app: choose **Done**, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll down and choose **Open Anyway** next to {P}, enter your Mac password and choose **Open Anyway** again. After that it opens normally.

The app opens in trial mode, with every feature, for {TRIAL} client binders. There is no account to create.

## Build your first binder

1. Choose **New binder** (or press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> on Windows, <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>K</Kbd> on a Mac, and type “new”).
2. Pick the client from the list, or choose **+ Add a new client** and type their name. Check the tax year, then choose **Start empty binder** (or drop the files right there first and choose **Start binder**).
3. Drag the client’s files or whole folders onto the window, or choose **Add files**. {P} accepts PDF, JPG, PNG, HEIC and multi-page TIFF files.

The processing view lists each file as it is read. Digital PDFs are read from their text layer; scans and phone photos are straightened and read with text recognition on your computer. A password-protected or damaged file gets its own card with the reason, and the rest of the pile carries on.

## Review

The review board groups documents by binder category, in your binder order. Documents {P} is sure about carry no marker. An amber dot means **Check**; a red dot means **Needs review**, and those documents sit at the top. Copies of a document you already have are set aside as duplicates and left out of the binder.

Work through the board with the keyboard: <Kbd>J</Kbd> and <Kbd>K</Kbd> move between documents, <Kbd>Enter</Kbd> accepts, and <Kbd>T</Kbd> changes the form type. The [review shortcuts](/docs/review-shortcuts/) page lists every key.

## Export

Choose **Export** (or press <Kbd>Ctrl</Kbd> <Kbd>E</Kbd>, <Kbd>⌘</Kbd> <Kbd>E</Kbd> on a Mac). The export dialog sets:

- **Binder order**: drag categories into your firm’s order. It is saved for every binder.
- **Contents**: a cover page, a clickable index, and optionally one renamed PDF per document.
- **Document names** and **Image quality** for scanned and photographed pages.
- **Save to**: the folder, remembered for each client. {P} never overwrites a file; a second export gets “(2)” in its name.

When the export finishes, choose **Show in Explorer** (Windows) or **Show in Finder** (macOS) to open the folder, or **Start next binder**.

## What’s missing from last year

From a client’s second year on, the review board compares the new pile with the documents in last year’s binder. A **Missing from** panel (for a 2026 binder, “Missing from 2025”) lists each form and issuer that hasn’t arrived yet. Mark one **Not expected this year** if the client closed that account, and choose **Copy missing-docs list** to paste a ready-to-send list into an email to the client.

{P} keeps only the form type and issuer of each exported document for this comparison: no names, amounts or identification numbers.
