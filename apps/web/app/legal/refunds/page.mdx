If {P} isn’t right for your practice, you get your money back. This page explains how.

## The policy

Request a refund within **{REFUND_DAYS} days** of your purchase date and we refund the full amount. You don’t need to give a reason.

## How to request one

Reply to your Lemon Squeezy receipt email, or write to <SupportEmail /> with your order number or the email address you used to buy. We process requests within two business days.

## What happens next

- The refund goes back to your original payment method. Your bank may take 5–10 business days to show it.
- The license key is disabled when the refund is issued. Copies of {P} activated with it return to trial mode the next time they check the key online.
- Binders you already exported are yours to keep.

## After {REFUND_DAYS} days

We review later requests case by case, for example when a purchase was made twice by mistake.
