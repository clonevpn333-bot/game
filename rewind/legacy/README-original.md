# rewind. — TikTok Time Machine

A local-first, single-file TikTok-style viewer for original posts from **2020** and **September–December 2023**.

## Open on your Mac

1. Double-click `rewind.html`. Chrome is recommended, though Safari may work.
2. Choose **Fall '23** or **2020** in the left menu (or use Filters on a phone).
3. Press **↓ / ↑** to move through videos, scroll the page outside the video player, use the on-screen **NEXT/PREV** buttons, or swipe along the **right edge** of the player on mobile.
4. Tap **＋** and paste authentic TikTok URLs to grow your archive. The video ID is checked to ensure its timestamp is from the chosen eras.
5. Favorite posts using ♥. Everything you add or hide is saved in this browser's local storage; use **Export library** to back it up.

**No installation, coding, account, or API key required.** An internet connection is required to watch the embedded videos.

## The current collection

- 40 original 2020 TikTok posts linked from TikTok's 2020 Year on TikTok roundup.
- 30 original uploads from September–December 2023, linked from contemporary trend roundups and archived trend documentation.
- The playlist loops to the beginning; it does **not** fetch an infinite number of new posts or reconstruct an old TikTok recommendation algorithm.

## Important limitations

The site embeds TikTok's **official hosted player**, not copied video files. TikTok may block playback, demand login, mute audio, remove a video, or limit embedding based on browser/region. Such videos cannot be recovered by changing your computer clock. Use **ORIGINAL** to try the post on TikTok, or **HIDE** to skip it in the local feed. The uploaded ID's creation timestamp is checked, but we could not playback-test every video in this environment.

The site has no server, trackers, ads, or analytics of its own. TikTok's embeds connect to TikTok's servers and may involve their cookies, trackers, and terms. This project is independent of TikTok.

## Origins of the links

See `seed-videos.json` for individual post IDs, the dates decoded from those IDs, creators where known, and links to the original research/reference pages:

- TikTok Newsroom's Year on TikTok 2020 Top 100
- Vamp's October and November 2023 trend roundups
- Know Your Meme's Roman Empire, Ceiling Challenge, Name a Woman, and Big Boots trend histories
- Les Gens d'Internet's December 2023 roundup

Some source links point only to the video ID, not a preserved username. Those use an official ID-based player link instead of inventing the creator's name.
