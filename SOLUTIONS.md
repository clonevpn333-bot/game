# HOLLOW TIDE — Solutions (spoilers)

Controls: **Move** WASD/arrows · **Sprint** Shift · **Crouch / sneak** hold C
(crouch in place, or move while held to creep quietly) · **Jump / mantle /
let go** Space · **Grab · interact · climb · drag** E (hold to drag).
Raise the lantern with **Q** — it lights more, but the Warden's eye is drawn to it.

Movable crates are the ones bound with **pale rope lashings** — that's the only
tell, no glow. Everything else is scenery.

---

## ACT I — THE BEACH OF HULLS

**1 · Tideline.** Walk right. You climb the riveted hull bulkhead: push the
rope-lashed **crate** against it (hold E to drag), climb crate → jump at the wall
→ Space to mantle up. The **lantern sits on the crate you climb** — you pick it up
just by reaching it. Head right into the glowing hull breach.
*Hidden stub #1: far left at the start, behind the first ship-rib.*

**2 · The Hull.** Drag the rope-lashed crate to the tall ledge. Climb crate → grab
the mid ledge (Space near it) → mantle → jump-grab the upper ledge. From its right
edge, run off and jump — you auto-catch the hanging **hook**. Pump with A/D, release
at the top of a forward swing (Space) to the second hook, then to the far floor.
Black water = death.
*Stub #2: crawl (hold C) under the low crate near the entrance.*

**3 · Ribs.** Two long hook-chains over deep water. Run off the deck, jump into hook
one, swing, release to hook two, release to the far deck. Then **pull the hanging
chain (E)** by the iron door — the pulley above winches it open.
*Stub #3: climb the giant stool (jump to its rung, then the seat).*

## ACT II — THE BRINE CELLARS

**4 · Salt Store.** Mid-room the **Warden** crosses once. Duck under the giant work
table (walk into its shadow; don't sprint — under furniture = hidden). Wait for him
to shuffle off left, then go. *Stub #4: far right corner, on the floor.*

**5 · Jar Rows** *(the tall vault — the main stealth room).* The Warden patrols and
is **busy at his rounds** (stooping, groping the dark) when he pauses. Rules: **metal
grates are loud, cloth runners are silent** — creep (hold C) across grates, hide
under the raised crates, and **kick a can** (walk into it) to send him the wrong way.
The lock: **drag the giant key (E) from the floor near the entrance all the way to
the right-hand door.** It's heavy and noisy, so move it in bursts and let go to hide
whenever he turns. With the key beside the door, **press E at the door** to unlock.
*Stub #5: step through the hanging cloth curtain, far right.*

**6 · The Chute.** A trap room. Passing the stacked jars topples them and **wakes the
Warden into a hunt — sprint right immediately** (Shift): under the low table, over
the small crates, and at the far wall a **low slit at floor level** — run at it and
you auto-crawl through. He can't follow. It's a straight sprint; don't stop.
*Stub #6: front-left, grab it before triggering the jars.*

## ACT III — THE TALLOW WORKS

The blind **Renderer twins** can't see or hear — **they feel your footsteps through
the floor.** Sprinting is felt far, walking mid-range, creeping (C) barely.
**Standing still = invisible. Steam vents mask you. On the belt / in the air =
invisible.**

**7 · Rendering Floor** *(industrial cathedral — huge).* Move steam-pocket to
steam-pocket, freezing when the twin sweeps near. At the far right, **pull the wall
lever (E)** — the clank draws it to that spot, so pull and immediately creep away
into the last steam vent. The hatch grinds open. *Stub #7: shadow under the first vat.*

**8 · Conveyors.** The second twin owns the far shore; your shore is safe. **Flip the
left lever (E) to start the belt,** climb the metal steps, and **ride the belt across
the pit** (the belt hides your steps). A tallow brick rides ahead and drops off the
end onto the **weight plate**, raising the exit gate. Hop off into the steam near the
gate and slip through while the twin is on its far loop. *Stub #8: far-right corner.*

**9 · The Vats.** Both twins, one per half. **Drag the two tallow blocks onto the two
front plates** (one block far left, one far right). Work in bursts, freezing when a
twin turns; rest in the two steam trenches. Both plates weighted → the grate rises.
The grinding pulls both twins toward it, so dash through or wait ~5s in steam.

## ACT IV — THE LONG STAIR

**10 · Procession** *(the grand reveal — the widest shot in the game).* Grey figures
shuffle in two lanes (up the back, down the front). **They ignore you unless you
stand directly in a walker's path** for about a second — then they take you. Climb
each giant step (jump-grab-mantle); where jars block the middle, slip through a gap
between walkers. *Stub #9 (the last one): top landing, far back corner.*

**11 · The Gallery.** The Warden paces below; the exit is held shut by a
counterweight. A **ballast crate hangs over the middle of his route**, and he pauses
beneath it each loop. Route to the release: climb the **left chain** (E to grab, W to
climb, Space to hop off) → jump to the middle platform → from its right edge **jump
and press E in mid-air to catch the second chain** → climb to the top platform → the
**release lever**. When he stops under the crate, **pull it (E)** — it drops and pins
him, and the gate opens. Miss and it re-winches after a few seconds; try again. (You
can also stand on the middle platform and **hold Q** to lure him underneath.)

## ACT V — THE LANTERN ROOM

**12 · Stairhead.** Safe. The shrine shows every stub you found (lit = collected).
The door opens as you approach.

**13 · The Lens** *(cathedral finale — the biggest space).* The Warden circles the
lens. Two valve wheels aim the great beam: the **left valve turns it one way, the
right the other, 30° per press of E.** Two things:
1. **Whenever the beam crosses the Warden, he freezes.** Park it on his path to pin
   him while you cross. Never idle near his route — up close he feels you even still.
2. **Turn the RIGHT valve exactly 4 times** (or the left 8) to point the beam at the
   great doors. Held on them ~3 seconds, they char and swing open. Walk right, into
   the white. That is the ending.

---

**Survival notes.** Sprinting tires her (she refuses when spent — the slump is the
tell). Big drops land loud. Death costs about a second and respawns you at the room's
entrance with that room's puzzle reset. Progress auto-saves per room, so **CONTINUE**
resumes where you fell.
