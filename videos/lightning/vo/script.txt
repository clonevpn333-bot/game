This man was struck by lightning seven times.
And he swore a storm cloud chased him.
Roy Sullivan was a park ranger. Lightning found him everywhere.
In a fire tower. In his truck. In his own front yard.
It burned off his eyebrows, and set his hair on fire. Twice.
One day he saw a dark cloud, and drove away. It followed him.
He stepped out of the truck, and the bolt hit.
Even his wife got struck, hanging laundry.
Strike seven: he was fishing. Then a bear tried to steal his trout.
Would you stand next to him in a storm?
