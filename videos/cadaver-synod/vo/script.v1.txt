This pope had been dead for nine months... when he went on trial.
Rome, 897. The new pope, Stephen the Sixth, hated the old one.
So he dug up the corpse, dressed it in robes, and sat it on a throne.
A deacon spoke for it, while Stephen screamed at the corpse.
The verdict? Guilty.
They cut off the three fingers he blessed with, and threw his body in the river.
Rome was horrified. Within months, Stephen was in prison... and strangled.
History calls it the Cadaver Synod. The day a corpse was put on trial.
