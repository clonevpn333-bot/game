She fell 33,000 feet. No parachute.
And she was never supposed to be on that plane.
The airline confused her with another stewardess named Vesna.
In 1972, a blast tore her jet apart.
Everyone else died. She fell, pinned inside the wreckage by a food cart.
It hit a snowy forest. A villager heard screaming.
Fractured skull. Broken legs. Alive.
She set the world record: the highest fall ever survived.
But decades later, two journalists said that record... was a lie.
