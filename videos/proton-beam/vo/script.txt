A particle beam went straight through this man's head.
The Soviet Union, 1978.
Anatoli Bugorski leaned into a particle accelerator to check a broken part.
But the safety system had failed. The beam was still on.
A flash, brighter than a thousand suns. And no pain.
The dose was hundreds of times what kills a human.
Half his face swelled up. Doctors sent him to a clinic, to die.
He didn't. He finished his PhD, and went back to work. At the same machine.
He's the only person known to have been hit by a particle beam.
