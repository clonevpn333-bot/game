A proton beam shot through his head.
Soviet Union, 1978.
Anatoli Bugorski leaned into the machine to check a broken part.
The safety system had failed. The beam was still on.
A flash, brighter than a thousand suns. He felt nothing.
The dose was enormous. Half his face swelled up.
Doctors sent him to a clinic, to die.
He didn't.
He finished his PhD, and went back to work, at the same machine.
He's the only person known to have been hit by a particle beam.
