In July 1518, a woman stepped into a street in Strasbourg... and began to dance.
No music. She just couldn't stop.
Within days, more than thirty people were dancing. By the end, nearly four hundred.
The city's cure? More dancing. They built stages and hired musicians.
It only made it worse.
Was it poison? Ergot, a fungus on damp rye, can cause spasms and hallucinations.
But most historians suspect fear: a city starving, sick, and terrified of a curse.
Fear, it seems, can make a body dance.
