This prisoner's face was hidden for thirty-four years.
And nobody knows who he was.
In 1669, the King of France locked him away in secret.
His jailer was told: if he speaks about anything but his needs, kill him.
Four prisons. One jailer. Always masked.
He died in the Bastille in 1703, under a fake name.
Then they burned everything he owned.
Some believed he was the king's secret twin.
But the strangest part? The iron mask was never iron. It was black velvet.
