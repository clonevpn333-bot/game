A man was murdered... with an umbrella.
London, 1978. An exiled writer waits for a bus.
A sharp sting in his thigh. A stranger with an umbrella... gone.
Four days later, Georgi Markov was dead.
But the real weapon was smaller than a pinhead.
A platinum pellet, drilled with two holes, sealed to melt at body heat.
Inside: ricin. A castor-bean poison that stops cells making proteins.
The pellet was found in his thigh. Investigators believe the umbrella was a gun.
No one was ever charged.
The murder weapon was one point seven millimetres wide.
