Near a black hole, you wouldn't see darkness first. You'd see the future.
Gravity here is so strong, it bends light. The stars behind it smear into a glowing ring.
Now hover, just above the edge. Your watch ticks normally. To you.
But look back at Earth. Its clocks are racing. Every minute for you is years down there.
Cities spread. Seasons flicker. You're watching the future, on fast forward.
Now let go. And fall. Gravity pulls on your feet harder than your head.
So you stretch. Scientists really call this, spaghettification.
Past the edge, things get weird. Space and time trade places.
The center isn't a place anymore. It's a moment, in your future. Every path leads there.
You can't turn back, any more than you can turn back time.
And what's waiting at the very center? Nobody knows. Our physics just... stops.
