# HOMO CURIOSUS — STYLE

The channel: a universe of knowledge about the creativity of the human race — discovering more about humans,
civilization, and everything. Science, mystery and history colliding.

## Visual DNA (permanent)

| Role | Name | Hex |
|---|---|---|

| Light plate | warm ivory | #F2E8D0 |

## The recurring motif: THE CURIOSITY LINE

One continuous line — burnt-orange ink on ivory, curiosity-yellow light on navy — that becomes things:
orbit, timeline, crack, DNA, path, graph, constellation, waveform, fingerprint, question mark, letter.
Use it often enough to feel intentional, never so often that it is wallpaper. Canonical implementation:
`homo-curiosus-origin/pf/src/cast.js` (`FILM.hc.line`).

## Recurring visual language

- curiosity: the question mark, the reaching hand, the line that keeps going
- diagrams transforming into reality (blueprint ⇄ illustration match cuts)
- hidden systems revealed (strata, lattices, gear trains, orbits, cutaways)
- visual scale changes (push to macro, pull back to cosmic)
- hand-ink imperfection: boiling lines on twos, hatching not gradients, paper grain

## Never

UI cards, centred title cards, generic icons, emoji, gradient blobs, drop-shadow web design, slideshows,
repetitive fade/scale entrances, everything centred.

## Typography (max two systems)

- Display / captions: **Fraunces** 600 (warm serif), ivory with ink stroke on paper, ivory with navy glow on navy; spoken word lit in curiosity yellow.
- Annotation: **JetBrains Mono** 500, small caps, tracking 0.18em, ≤ 3 labels per shot.
- The wordmark HOMO CURIOSUS is drawn by the curiosity line (monoline engraved capitals), never typeset.
