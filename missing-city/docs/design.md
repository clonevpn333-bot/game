# THE MISSING CITY — Design Brief

## Player promise
You walk into a city that vanished eleven years ago with everyone in it — and slowly realise the city has been waiting for *you*.

## Target feeling
Eerie normality → dread → wonder → grief → catharsis. "I need to know what happened here."

## Verbs
- **Primary:** explore / observe (walk, look, flashlight, interact with the remains of 2:17 AM).
- **Signature:** ECHO — step into a replay of the city's final day for a few seconds (bridges, codes, elevators, walls that did not exist yet).
- **Secondary:** sprint, crouch, jump, vault, mantle, climb ladders, push crates, pistol (aim/shoot/reload), melee, dodge roll, stealth takedowns, talk to companions, read documents, enter keypad codes.

## Loop
- 5–30 s: move through an authored space, notice something (light, sound, ghost, document), interact or Echo.
- 1–5 min: a beat resolves — a door opens, a revelation, a set piece, an encounter, a puzzle.
- Chapter: one location, one emotional turn, one mystery answer that opens the next question.

## Core loop contract
Player **explores and uses Echoes** to **push deeper toward the truth of 2:17 AM** while **Remnants, unstable reality and a collapsing Echo timer** create risk; success **opens the next location and reveals story**, failure **returns you to the last checkpoint seconds earlier** (no long reloads).

## Pressure & failure
Remnants (7 behaviours), falling when an Echo collapses beneath you, collapse sequences. Death → fade → checkpoint retry of the current block only.

## What a better player does
Uses Echo timing efficiently, stealths past blind Remnants instead of fighting, headshots, dodges telegraphed lunges, finds every document.

## Communication of next decision
Companion lines, subtitles, lit landmarks, objective toast (top-left, fades), interaction prompts in world, Echo shimmer on objects that changed since 2:17.

## Non-goals
Open world, loot/crafting, multiplayer, mobile touch controls (desktop keyboard/mouse + gamepad basics).

## Art direction
Wet asphalt, sodium + neon contrast (amber/teal/magenta), rain streaks, volumetric light cones, deep shadow, everything still *on*. Echo = warm sepia/cyan replay with scanline shimmer. Late chapters invert the palette (violet skies, white geometry, gravity-wrong architecture).
