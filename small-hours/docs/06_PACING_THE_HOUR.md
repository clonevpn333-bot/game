# 06 — PACING THE HOUR
### Minute-by-minute beat sheet for the ~60-minute playthrough

Timing is the difference between "slow burn" and "boring." This is the fuse,
calibrated so dread compounds and the player never has two quiet beats in a row
after the midpoint. Times are approximate target windows for a first-time player.

Legend: 🟦 awake-in-clinic · 🟪 under (dream/paralysis) · 🔋 phone beat ·
🩺 EEG/dread-meter beat · 🎬 filter-escalation beat · ⭐ Surprise/Profiler beat.

---

---

### Pacing rules used here
1. **No two calm beats in a row after 0:30.** Once the fuse is lit, every quiet
   moment is immediately undercut.
2. **Teach safe, punish later.** The first dream and first paralysis are
   survivable and even gentle, so the player *trusts* the mechanic — then it turns.
3. **One instrument the player learns to trust (the clock/EEG), then break it.**
   The faked wake only works because we spent 20 minutes making the clock reliable.
4. **The reveal is assembled, not narrated.** Act IV gives the player props, not a
   monologue; understanding dawns *on them,* which is scarier and more *Fears to
   Fathom.*
5. **The last 60 seconds do the most work.** The ending and the Fear Profile are
   the part players quote to friends — they get the most polish per second.
